# Maheshwari Counsel — Supreme Court Official Source Map

## Purpose

This file defines the authoritative Supreme Court of India web sources that the Maheshwari Counsel content system may use for legal-awareness research and verification.

**Important:** This is a source registry, not a copied database of the Court's entire website or judgment corpus. For a specific legal topic, the system must retrieve and verify the relevant original judgment/order/rule before generating content.

## 1. Supreme Court Primary Portal

- https://www.sci.gov.in/
- Provides official access to judgments, orders, case status, daily orders, cause lists, advocates' resources, court rules, guidelines/orders and other Court information.

## 2. Judgments

- https://www.sci.gov.in/judgements/
- Use for the original Supreme Court judgment relevant to a topic.
- Do not rely on an AI-generated case citation or an unverified secondary summary when the original judgment is available.

## 3. Landmark Judgment Summaries

- https://www.sci.gov.in/landmark-judgment-summaries/
- Useful for discovering important judgments and understanding their subjects.
- The Court expressly states that these summaries are prepared for better understanding, do not form part of the Court's decision/reasons, and are not for use in legal proceedings.
- Therefore: summary = discovery/context; original judgment = legal authority.

## 4. Supreme Court Rules

- https://www.sci.gov.in/supreme-court-rules/
- Current Supreme Court Rules and amendment notifications are listed here.
- The source currently lists the Supreme Court Rules, 2013 and subsequent amendment notifications, including 2024 and 2025 amendments.

## 5. Guidelines & Orders

- https://www.sci.gov.in/guidelines-orders/
- Use for official Court-issued guidelines, directions and orders.
- This currently includes, among other material, 2026 bail-bonds-related rules/guidelines and PIL Guidelines.

## 6. Constitution / Court Institutional Information

- https://www.sci.gov.in/constitution/
- Use for the Court's official explanatory material on constitutional structure, sources of law, judiciary, Supreme Court jurisdictional/institutional information and advocates before the Supreme Court.

## 7. Supreme Court Search / Reports

- https://scr.sci.gov.in/scrsearch/
- Official Supreme Court Reports search portal.
- Supports searching judgments/orders using keywords, Acts, sections, party names, judges and other search criteria.

## 8. Case Status / Daily Orders / Proceedings

- https://www.sci.gov.in/case-status-court/
- Use only when the content requires current case-status or procedural information.
- Current case status must never be inferred from an old judgment or secondary article.

## 9. Supreme Court FAQ / Research Navigation

- https://www.sci.gov.in/faq-ready-reckoner-tips-for-obtaining-information-relating-to-supreme-court/
- Official navigation guidance for obtaining certified records, searching/downloading judgments, daily orders/proceedings, case status, PIL guidelines, Supreme Court Rules and practice/procedure material.

## 10. Official Practice & Procedure Material

The Supreme Court website links to its official Practice and Procedure material and other Registry resources. Use the current official version rather than an archived third-party copy.

## 11. Source Hierarchy for Maheshwari Counsel

When preparing a legal-awareness reel, use this priority order:

1. Current statutory text from an authoritative legislative source (prefer India Code / official Gazette where applicable).
2. Original Supreme Court judgment/order when judicial interpretation is required.
3. Current Supreme Court Rules / official guidelines where Court procedure is involved.
4. Current High Court judgment/order where the proposition specifically concerns a High Court or Delhi jurisdiction.
5. Official government/regulator material for administrative or regulatory facts.
6. Supreme Court landmark summaries only as discovery/context; verify against the original judgment.
7. Third-party bare-act/education sites such as Your Lordships only as a discovery/convenience source, never as the final authority when an official source is available.

## 12. Mandatory AI Verification Rule

Before generating a Maheshwari Counsel reel:

- Identify the exact legal proposition being stated.
- Identify the applicable Act/section/rule or judgment where relevant.
- Verify the current position from the authoritative source.
- Record the source title, section/order/paragraph and date where practical.
- If the proposition cannot be verified, STOP and mark it `VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`.
- Never fill a missing legal proposition from model memory.
- Never manufacture a case citation, paragraph number, section, penalty, limitation period, procedural requirement or legal conclusion.

## 13. Reel Safety Boundary

The reel system is for legal education and professional awareness only. It must not convert source material into direct or indirect client solicitation, guarantees, case-result marketing, testimonials or superiority claims. Existing BCI professional-conduct guardrails remain mandatory.

## 14. Research Coverage

The Supreme Court website contains a large and continuously changing corpus of judgments, orders, notices, rules and other materials. The system should therefore use **topic-based retrieval and current-source verification**, rather than attempting to freeze the entire website into a static local copy.

This design is intentional: a static corpus can become outdated when statutes, rules or judicial positions change.
