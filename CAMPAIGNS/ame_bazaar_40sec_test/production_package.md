# AME BAZAAR — 40 SECOND REEL
**Campaign ID**: CAM_TEST_40SEC_001
**Topic**: Premium Showroom Saree Collection Promo

---

## 1. CAMPAIGN BRIEF

- **Campaign Goal**: Showcase AME Bazaar's premium retail showroom and the quality of its designer saree collection.
- **Target Audience**: Women shopping for wedding, bridal, and festive collections in North West Delhi (Kirari).
- **Featured Product**: Premium Silk Zari embroidery sarees.
- **Offer / Message**: "Custom tailoring & bridal draping designs at AME Bazaar, Mubarakpur Road."
- **Video Duration**: 40 seconds.
- **Platform**: Instagram Reels (9:16 vertical format).
- **Tone**: Elegant, premium, slow-paced, realistic fashion commercial.
- **Story Idea**: A customer walks into AME Bazaar, approaches the premium sarees display racks under warm accent spotlights, feels the texture, walks toward checkout, and leaves with a branded shopping bag.
- **Call to Action**: Visit AME Bazaar at Mubarakpur Road, Kirari, Delhi or message via WhatsApp (+91 99535 69533) to request catalog details.

---

## 2. PRODUCTION PLAN

- **Timeline Integrity**: Total of 8 scenes, exactly 5.0 seconds per scene.
- **Story Arc**:
  - `Scene 01` (0-5s): Entrance hook. Camera tracks behind the customer entering the storefront.
  - `Scene 02` (5-10s): Interior reveal. Camera pans left to showcase the neat clothing racks.
  - `Scene 03` (10-15s): Product selection. Customer approaches the silk saree displays.
  - `Scene 04` (15-20s): Product close-up. Macro detail of the gold zari embroidery.
  - `Scene 05` (20-25s): Customer interaction. Smiling model holding the saree fabric up to check drape.
  - `Scene 06` (25-30s): Checkout / Billing. Customer walks to the checkout counter.
  - `Scene 07` (30-35s): Exit transition. Customer walks out of frame holding the eco-friendly branded paper bag.
  - `Scene 08` (35-40s): Call to action end-frame. Branded card, WhatsApp logo, and contact info overlay.

## 7. ASSEMBLY & HANDOFF INSTRUCTIONS

### Scene-to-Scene Transitions
- **Scene 01 → Scene 02**: Match cut on the model crossing the door threshold.
- **Scene 02 → Scene 03**: Spatial cut following the model's walking vector.
- **Scene 03 → Scene 04**: Direct cut on hand contact (wide to macro detail zoom).
- **Scene 04 → Scene 05**: Match-cut on texture drape transition.
- **Scene 05 → Scene 06**: Cut on space change to counter.
- **Scene 06 → Scene 07**: Movement cut on bag handoff to walk exit.
- **Scene 07 → Scene 08**: Fade out to clean branding CTA screen.

### Google Flow Upload Checklist
1. Select **9:16 Vertical Aspect Ratio** in your generation settings.
2. Under "Layer Overlays / Branding", load the logo asset `R001` (`logo.png`).
3. Set the motion rate slider to **Low / Medium (0.3-0.5)** to prevent unrealistic speed artifacts.
4. Input negative prompt: `CGI, 3D render, cartoon, robotic motion, teleports, wardrobe shifts`.

### Final Assembly Instructions
- Sequence scenes from `Scene 01` through `Scene 08` chronologically.
- Add soft cross-dissolve transitions of **0.2 seconds** at the boundary of Scene 07 to Scene 08.
- Overlay a soft traditional/acoustic instrumental audio track matched to the voice-over narrative.
